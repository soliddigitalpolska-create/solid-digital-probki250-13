What to check when Friday sales look wrong

One shop’s Friday revenue drops by 12%. Before changing the staff rota, check which measure moved. Revenue can fall because fewer baskets reached the till, because shoppers spent less per transaction, or both.

Start by comparing the same hours in two comparable weeks. Check that both stores reported a full day, then break revenue and transaction count down by hour. If the gap starts at 17:00, check late stockouts and till coverage before attributing it to demand.

Use the result to choose one test for next Friday. A useful dashboard narrows the decision to an action you can observe.

Two shops can both be down 12% on Friday and need completely different follow-up.

At the first shop, fewer transactions go through after 17:00. At the second, transaction count is flat, but basket value drops around lunch. The first deserves a look at availability and service coverage. The second deserves a look at item mix and discounts.

Before you change anything across the whole chain, compare like-for-like hours and write down one thing the store can test next week. Which measure would you open first?
