What I check before handoff

Composition, fit, model measurements and care instructions must match the source. I do not invent sustainability claims or product benefits. Headlines are localized for tone and intent, while product facts remain unchanged.

This exercise demonstrates writing and localization. It does not claim commercial fashion experience or measured sales results.
